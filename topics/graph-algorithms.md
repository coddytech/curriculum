# Graph Algorithms

Seven short, focused courses — implement each algorithm yourself in your language of choice (C, C++, C#, Dart, Go, Java, Lua, PHP, Python, R, Ruby, Rust or Swift). Every course earns a free certificate.

**Prerequisite:** [Graphs - Data Structures Series #9](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/graphs__data_structures_series_9) — know how to represent a graph before you traverse one. See the [DSA track](data-structures-and-algorithms.md).

Browse the full topic on Coddy: [Graph Algorithms Courses](https://coddy.tech/landing/topic/graph-algorithms-courses)

## The track, in order

Traversals first, then algorithms that build on them.

1. [Breadth-First Search](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/breadth_first_search__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/breadth-first-search)
2. [Depth-First Search](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/depth_first_search__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/depth-first-search)
3. [Topological Sort](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/topological_sort__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/topological-sort) *(builds on DFS)*
4. [Dijkstra's Algorithm](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/dijkstras_algorithm__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/dijkstras-algorithm) *(uses a priority queue — do [Heaps](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/heaps_and_priority_queues__data_structures_series_7) first)*
5. [Bellman-Ford Algorithm](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/bellman_ford_algorithm__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/bellman-ford-algorithm) *(shortest paths with negative weights)*
6. [Prim's Algorithm](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/prims_algorithm__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/prims-algorithm) *(minimum spanning trees)*
7. [Kruskal's Algorithm](https://coddy.tech/landing/courses/kruskals_algorithm__graph_algorithms) 🛠️ 🎓 — 9 lessons · [visualize](https://coddy.tech/visualize/graphs/kruskals-algorithm) *(minimum spanning trees, union-find style)*
