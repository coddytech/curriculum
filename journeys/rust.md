# Rust Journey

Rust delivers C-level performance with memory safety, and consistently ranks as the most loved language among developers. The journey takes you from `println!` to ownership, borrowing and traits.

**[Start the Rust journey →](https://coddy.tech/landing/rust)**

At ~30 minutes a day, most people finish in 4–8 weeks. 202 lessons across 3 sections. Finish every section to earn the free certificate.
