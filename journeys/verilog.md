# Verilog Journey

Verilog is the hardware description language used to design and verify digital circuits and chips. The journey covers modules, always blocks and the fundamentals of digital design, then RTL design, pipelines and self-checking testbenches.

**[Start the Verilog journey →](https://coddy.tech/landing/verilog)**

At ~30 minutes a day, most people finish in 4–8 weeks. 128 lessons across 2 sections.

## The journey

1. [Fundamentals](https://coddy.tech/landing/verilog#syllabus-section-fundamentals) — 90 lessons
2. [RTL Design & Verification](https://coddy.tech/landing/verilog#syllabus-section-rtl_design_and_verification) — 38 lessons
