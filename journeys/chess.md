# Chess Journeys

Coddy teaches chess the same way it teaches code: short interactive lessons, on the board, with every move checked. One journey takes you from the rules to your first real games; four topic journeys then go deep on endgames, tactics, openings and strategy. Browse them all at [coddy.tech/chess](https://coddy.tech/chess).

Sections marked 🔜 are planned and not yet live. Live sections link to their landing page.

## Openings 🔜

One section per opening family, from the systems a beginner can play to the theory a club player needs. Each section teaches the ideas, the main line, the common deviations, the tactics it produces and the middlegame it leads to, then has you play it against the bots. Every opening also has a reference page at [coddy.tech/chess/openings](https://coddy.tech/chess/openings).
