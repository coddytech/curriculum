# Interview Preparation

Ten packs of real interview-style coding challenges, in any language you like (C, C++, C#, Dart, Go, Java, Lua, PHP, Python, R, Ruby, Rust or Swift). Each pack is short by design — a warm-up you can finish in one sitting, ramping up as the numbers climb.

**Before you start:** you'll get much more out of these after the [Data Structures & Algorithms](data-structures-and-algorithms.md) track — most interview questions are data-structure questions in disguise.
