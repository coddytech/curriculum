# Solidity Journey

Solidity is the language of smart contracts on Ethereum and every EVM-compatible chain. The journey covers contract structure, typed state, mappings, structs, enums, events and `require` guards — every lesson compiles your contract and runs it on an EVM in the browser, no wallet or setup.

**[Start the Solidity journey →](https://coddy.tech/landing/solidity)**

At ~30 minutes a day, most people finish in 3–6 weeks. 53 lessons across 1 section. Finish every section to earn the free certificate.
