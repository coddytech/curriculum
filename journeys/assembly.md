# Assembly Journey

Assembly takes you to the metal: registers, memory and the instructions your CPU actually executes. The journey covers registers, instructions, memory and control flow at the lowest level, then integer arithmetic, loops, bit masks and indexed arrays.

**[Start the Assembly journey →](https://coddy.tech/landing/assembly)**

At ~30 minutes a day, most people finish in 4–8 weeks. 73 lessons across 2 sections. Finish every section to earn the free certificate.
