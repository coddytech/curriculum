# Kotlin Journey

Kotlin is the language Google recommends for Android, and a concise, null-safe choice for backend services with Spring Boot and Ktor. The journey takes you from `val` and `var` through null safety, `when`, functions and lists, then on to maps, sets, lambdas, collection pipelines and error handling, then classes, interfaces, and data and sealed classes.

**[Start the Kotlin journey →](https://coddy.tech/landing/kotlin)**

At ~30 minutes a day, most people finish in 4–8 weeks. 213 lessons across 3 sections. Finish every section to earn the free certificate.
